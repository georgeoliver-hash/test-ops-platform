# PV Suite — Coherence & Capability-Grounding Audit

**Scope:** all 133 cases in `suite_PV.json` (axio4 WinCE Platform Validator). Checked against FBD-100167 (multi-use barcodes), FBD-100317/100318 (Corethree/barcode config), FBD-100359 (SaaS/offline-failover), FBD-100304 (topology), FBD-100658 (ABT audit), FBD-100277 (card-reference/furthest-alighting), FBD-100720 (pilot list), FBD-100307/100229/100334 (ABT capping).

**Summary:** 9 cases flagged — **2 High** (impossible/contradicts spec + sibling case), **3 Med** (salvageable coherence/grounding), **4 Low** (minor / needs-confirmation). The bulk of the suite is clean and coherent; notably the ABT-audit cases (C4103565, C4101089, C4101092), the legacy-transfer pair (C4103566/67), the barcode-matrix reject case (C4103559) and the pilot-list trio (C4103562/63/64) are all well-grounded and internally consistent.

---

**C4101005** | High | Grounding + coherence | Title "Barcode — single-use validation" and step 1 assert **"present the valid single-use barcode → the PV validates it → shows the barcode success result."** FBD-100167 device matrix is explicit: **POS & TVM validate single-use only; PV validates MULTIPLE-use only.** A PV cannot successfully validate a single-use barcode. This directly contradicts sibling case **C4103559** in the same suite, which correctly has the PV *reject* a single-use barcode ("This Barcode Type is not accepted on this device"). | Retire or rewrite C4101005: the only valid PV single-use scenario is rejection (already covered by C4103559). Keep the multi-use case C4101006. Do not assert a success path for single-use on the PV.

**C4101085** | High | Grounding | Step 2: "the primary comms channel fails → **the PV fails over to the secondary communication method.**" Per FBD-100359 the PV is an **Ethernet** device routed via the Translink network; Ethernet devices (spec names "POS/PV/GV/Kiosk/T1 Dock") have **no failover** — on sustained loss they operate offline then enter a **communication-locked, out-of-service** state (only cellular-direct devices are unaffected). This asserts a capability the PV does not have, and contradicts sibling **C4103568**, which correctly models the Ethernet PV going comms-locked/out-of-service with no failover. | Drop the "secondary comms failover" step. Retain step 1 (offline + queue) and step 3 (queued data uploads on recovery); align the sustained-outage behaviour with C4103568.

**C4101078** | Med | Coherence + grounding | Three problems vs FBD-100720: (a) precond/step 1 "apply the pilot list (pilotlist.zip) to the PV via TMS, **including registration mode**" — the spec makes **registration mode and pilot mode mutually exclusive datasets** (`Enable Registration Mode=True, Enable Pilot List=False` vs the reverse); a single case cannot be "pilot list applied, including registration mode." (b) The list is imported as **binary `pilotlist.dat` into the `Pilot List File` field**, not applied as a "pilotlist.zip." (c) Step 3 "export the pilot list … **converts from CSV to DAT and downloads as CSV from Device Log Manager**" is garbled: it is the **registration file** that uploads (as a zip) to **CloudFare Device Logging**, and the **CSV↔binary round-trip is a back-office Arrive-personnel tool step (Pilot List Converter)**, not a device/TMS export. | Split into the two spec modes already covered cleanly by C4103562/63 (pilot validation) and C4103564 (registration upload at EoD); or rewrite as one distribution-only case. Fix artefact names (`pilotlist.dat` binary; "Device Logging").

**C4100991** | Med | Coherence | EA Smartpass. Preconditions bundle several failure modes — "at a weekend / during a school holiday / **before start / after expiry** / outside the valid time" — but step 2's single expected outcome is **"Invalid Time Of Day" reject screen**. An after-expiry pass yields **Product Expired**, and a before-start pass a different reason; the expected does not correspond to all the seeded conditions. | Narrow the case to the time-of-day dimension (weekend/holiday/outside band → Invalid Time Of Day), and move before-start/after-expiry to the expiry-reason case (C4100992 already parametrises reason screens).

**C4101095** | Med | Grounding (needs-confirmation) | Step 2 asserts the PV reports a **"SIM ID"** to CloudFare (visible in Asset Manager). A SIM implies a cellular link, but the comms model treats the PV as **Ethernet-only** (FBD-100359/100304; static IP 192.168.3.10 in C4103568/C4101016). Whether the axio4 PV carries a modem/SIM is not established in the specs reviewed. | Confirm PV hardware comms. If Ethernet-only, replace "SIM ID" with the applicable network/asset identifier; if a modem exists, this also affects the C4101085 failover finding — reconcile the two.

**C4102429** | Low | Needs-confirmation | Expected: the rail ABT success screen "displays the additional line **'Northern Ireland Travel Only'**." A rail-specific ABT success screen does exist (design C4101036 "ABT Tag successful - Rail"), but the exact label text is not confirmed in the specs reviewed. | Confirm the label against the approved screen design before treating the exact string as an assertion.

**C4101088** | Low | Needs-confirmation | Step 3 lists "**cEMV is disabled in the Technician Menu**" as a disabling condition. None of the enumerated Technician-Menu functional cases (C4101011–C4101017, C4101091, C4102166–C4102181) expose a cEMV enable/disable toggle. | Confirm a Technician-Menu cEMV toggle exists; if not, drop that clause (route-attribute/location/fare checks already cover enablement).

**C4101012** | Low | Coherence | Precondition states "a technician is signed in … **with back-office comms available**," yet step 3 exercises the **comms-unavailable** path ("Failed to retrieve locations from server"). The precondition and the step condition conflict. | Reword the precondition to be comms-neutral, or move the comms-down sub-step to its own case so the setup matches the action.

**C4101090** | Low | Needs-confirmation | Step 2 expects a **"No Days Left"** outcome screen; the enumerated approved-screen set (C4101023–C4101071) does not include a "No Days Left"/"No Journeys Left" screen (cf. C4100986 which also uses "No Journeys Left"). The reject-reason screen naming may be legitimate but is unverified against the design set. | Confirm the exact reject-screen name used for exhausted period/journey products and align C4100986/C4101090 wording to it.
