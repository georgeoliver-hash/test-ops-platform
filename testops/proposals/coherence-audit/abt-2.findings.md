# ABT suite coherence & capability audit — second half (indices 199–396)

**Scope:** 163 cases audited (suite_ABT.json indices 199–396, minus the 35 annulment/cancellation-section ids handled separately in annul_section.json). Checked for internal coherence (title↔preface↔preconds↔steps↔expected) and capability grounding against the FBD specs (100340 town services, 100389 ABT scenarios, 100662 Ulsterbus TOO, 100690 NIR TOTO, 100334 capping, back-office 100263/276/306/356).

**Summary:** 6 genuine findings (0 High, 2 Medium, 4 Low). The remainder are clean. No audited case invents a portal "cancel"/"annul" action, no capping-£0.00 vs cancellation-£0.00 conflation was found in this half, and the reference-fare / Update-Stop correction cases, NIR TOTO cases, capping cases, debt-recovery, report and portal-function cases are all coherent and grounded. (The "cancel in the Operator Portal" defects live in the annul_section set, which was excluded per instruction.)

---

**C4102789** | Medium | coherence + grounding | Town-service cap. Precond: two 203b (IN) Bangor TS taps capped to the £2.50 daily town cap. Step corrects *one* tap's alighting to a stop **outside** the Bangor TS zone; expected says the town cap is "no longer applied, **as both taps are no longer in-zone**". Only one tap was moved out of zone — the other remains in-zone. Per FBD-100340 the town cap is a per-town **daily cap** applied to journeys whose board+alight are both in-zone; a single remaining in-zone journey (£2.90 here) should still cap at £2.50, so "cap no longer applied" is not established and the stated reason ("both taps no longer in-zone") is factually wrong. | Rewrite the expected: the corrected out-of-zone leg stands uncapped (board-in/alight-out ⇒ not capped, per spec §33), and the remaining in-zone leg still caps at £2.50; day total = out-of-zone fare + £2.50. Fix the "both taps no longer in-zone" justification.

**C4102790** | Medium | grounding (unconstructible) + coherence | Town-service cap, correction outside town to a **lower** fare below £2.50. The precond itself admits the required data may not exist: "confirm the exact stop in the portal, as the reference's listed 203b (IN) out-of-zone pairs are all £4.50 or higher." A test whose scenario cannot be built from known fare data is not executable as written. Also carries the same "town service cap no longer applied" claim as C4102789, which is questionable for the remaining in-zone leg. | Confirm whether a sub-£2.50 out-of-zone 203b stop actually exists; if not, drop or re-base the case. Correct the cap-removal reasoning as for C4102789.

**C4102817** | Low | needs-confirmation | Duplicate detection, out-of-order upload. A buffered same-stage tap uploads with an **08:58** timestamp — earlier than the 09:00 tap the back office already treated as the "original" — and the expected marks the 08:58 tap as a Duplicate "linked to the 09:00 original". Whether the earliest-timestamp tap re-bases as the charged first tap, or arrival/processing order wins, is a real design question not confirmed in FBD-100389/100662. | Confirm the intended out-of-order rule with the ABT team; annotate the expected with the source once confirmed.

**C4102948** | Low | coherence (loose wording) | ETM Metro TOO end-to-end. Step 1 is a single tap; Step 2 expected states "the day is capped at £4.00". A single £2.30 Metro tap does not reach the £4.00 cap. The phrasing reads as an assertion the day hit the cap when it only states the cap rule value. | Reword to "the journey is charged £2.30 under the £4.00 Metro cap rule (cap not reached on one tap)", or add taps to genuinely reach the cap.

**C4103478** | Low | grounding (wording) | "A single Metro tap does not trigger the Metro cap" — preface/preconds state "the cap **needs two in-zone taps**". The Metro cap is a daily spend cap (£4.00); a lone £2.30 tap is simply below the threshold, not blocked by a "two-tap requirement". Outcome (charged £2.30, no cap) is correct; the mechanism stated is a mischaracterisation. | Reword to "a single £2.30 tap is below the £4.00 daily cap so no cap applies" rather than implying a minimum tap count.

**C4102854** | Low | coherence (terminology) | "The recovered account is visible … with the correct status" expects status **"ACCEPTED"**, whereas the sibling debt-recovery cases (C4102850/851/853) use **"Active"/"Blocked"**. If ACCEPTED is a distinct account-record field it is fine; if it is meant to be the same card status it is inconsistent. | Confirm whether the Customers-page masked-PAN account record uses "ACCEPTED" as a separate status value; align terminology with the other recovery cases if it is the same field.
