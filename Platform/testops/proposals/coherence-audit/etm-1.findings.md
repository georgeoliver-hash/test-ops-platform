# ETM Coherence & Grounding Audit — Part 1 (indices 0–227)

**Scope:** WinCE ETM suite (`suite_ETM.json`), array indices 0–227 inclusive (228 cases: Smoke through the Screen-Validation block).
**Method:** Each case checked for internal coherence (title ↔ preface ↔ preconds ↔ steps ↔ expected) and capability grounding against `knowledge/translink/specs/FBD-*.md` (principally FBD-100662 Ulsterbus TOO, FBD-100658 ABT Audit, FBD-100335 Rail Substitution).
**Result:** 8 cases flagged (2 High, 1 Medium, 5 Low). The remaining ~220 cases — including the entire Screen-Validation block (IDX 168–227), sign-on/off, sales, validation, concessionary/staff passes, top-up, capping, power, comms and menu families — read as internally coherent and consistent with the specs; no problems worth raising.
**Note:** The source JSON is clean UTF-8 (em-dash / £ / € verified); apparent mojibake in intermediate dumps was an extraction artefact only — **no encoding finding**.

## Findings

**C4100586** (IDX 91, ABT — Metro zone boarding and alighting) | High | grounding: action does not exist on this surface | The case has the customer "make a boarding-only tap, then an alighting-only tap … records the boarding-and-alighting pair." The Metro ETM is a **Tap-On-Only (TOO)** device (FBD-100658 scope: TOO = Metro ETM / Ulsterbus ETM; TOTO = NIR GV/PV/HHD), and its alighting location is **ETM-calculated** (last stop within the Metro Network Zone — FBD-100658 §Alighting-location). There is no customer alighting tap on the ETM; this conflates TOTO tap-on/tap-off mechanics onto a TOO device. | Recommend: rewrite to a TOO tap that audits with the calculated alighting stage, OR re-scope this case to a TOTO device (GV/PV). Remove the "alighting-only tap" / "boarding-and-alighting pair" actions.

**C4100587** (IDX 92, ABT — Ulsterbus zone boarding and alighting) | High | grounding: action does not exist on this surface | Same defect as C4100586 for the Ulsterbus ETM. Ulsterbus ETM is TOO with a **driver-selected** alighting stage (FBD-100662 UX flow §3–5; FBD-100658 "Ulsterbus ETM: driver does choose alighting stage"). A customer "alighting-only tap" and a recorded "boarding-and-alighting pair" are not real ETM capabilities. | Recommend: rewrite as a TOO tap with driver-selected alighting (matching FBD-100662), or move the tap-off behaviour to the correct TOTO device.

**C4100588** (IDX 93, ABT — reverts to standard fixed fare on rule change) | Medium | grounding: likely contradicts current fare model | On Ulsterbus route 72b the expected outcome is "the ABT journey reverts to the **standard fixed fare**." FBD-100662 explicitly **supersedes the old Metro flat-fare rule for Ulsterbus routes** — Ulsterbus now uses driver-selected-alighting TOO business rules (flat fare is retained for Metro routes only). A "fixed/flat fare" fallback on an Ulsterbus route is at odds with the current spec direction. | Recommend: confirm the intended fallback behaviour for Ulsterbus rule-change; if it is the driver-selected TOO fare, correct the expected result. Mark needs-confirmation against FBD-100662.

**C4103547** (IDX 137, Rail Substitution — tap audited with TransportationType "Rail Sub") | Low | needs-confirmation: feature gated off | Assertions (paymentType "ABT", revenue 0, TransportationType "Rail Sub") match FBD-100658/100662. However both specs state rail-substitution ABT is **kept OFF via the route ABT Type until NIR TOTO is implemented** (FBD-100662 §Rail Substitution). Case is coherent as designed but may not be executable in the current build. | Recommend: add a precondition/flag noting rail-sub ABT enablement, or tag as blocked pending NIR TOTO.

**C4103548** (IDX 138, Rail Substitution — back office ignores PJT but applies MJT) | Low | needs-confirmation: unverified assertion + gated feature | The split-one-TOO-tap-into-two-TOTO-taps (equal times) and **PJT ignored** match FBD-100662. The added clause "**Maximum Journey Time is still applied**" is **not present in the distilled spec** (spec mentions only PJT ignored). Same gated-until-NIR-TOTO caveat as C4103547. | Recommend: verify the MJT assertion against the full FBD-100662/100690 text; mark needs-confirmation.

**C4100550** (IDX 34, Ticket Issue — issue a single ticket) | Low | coherence: unrelated action tacked onto expected | Step-1 WHEN is "build ticket via fare look-up, tender more than the fare, take payment." The THEN correctly asserts the ticket prints and a change receipt prints, then adds "**and the change receipt can be annulled**" — an annulment assertion that is not exercised by the step and belongs to the annulment case (C4100551). Mild conflation of two mechanics. | Recommend: drop the annul clause from this expected result (it is covered by C4100551), or add an explicit annul step if genuinely intended here.

**C4100545** (IDX 29, FLU — currency switch to Euro and back) | Low | needs-confirmation: specific mechanism unverified | Preface/steps claim the **alighting-stage key** toggles the sale currency between Pounds and Euro. The Euro currency state itself is real (screen `07_0_4 Main Screen - Currency is Euro`; and Driver Menu currency change in C4100606), but that the *alighting-stage key* is the toggle is an unsourced mechanism claim. | Recommend: confirm the key mapping against the FLU/currency design; if unconfirmed, describe the action generically ("switch currency to Euro") rather than naming the key.

**C4102566 / C4102567** (IDX 49 & 55, Fare-Paying Smartcard — validation & top-up) | Low | needs-confirmation: product may not exist | Both cases assume a **stored-value ("fare-paying") smartcard that deducts the fare from an on-card balance**. Translink smartcards in the other cases are period/journey products (Travelcard, iLink, DayLink, Multi-Journey); pay-as-you-go is handled by ABT contactless. An on-card e-purse deduct model is not corroborated by the reviewed specs. | Recommend: confirm a stored-value/e-purse fare-paying smartcard product exists for the Translink ETM. If it does not, these cases are testing an invented capability and should be removed or re-scoped to ABT PAYG.
