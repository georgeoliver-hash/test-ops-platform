# HHD Coherence & Capability-Grounding Audit — findings

**Scope:** all 211 HHD cases (IDs 4103800–4104010) in `suite_HHD.json`, checked for internal coherence (title/preface/preconds/steps/expected describe the same scenario) and capability grounding against FBD-100716 (Revenue Inspection), FBD-100658 (ABT Audit), FBD-100662 (Ulsterbus TOO), FBD-100483 (Integrated Barcode API), FBD-100167 (Multiple-Use Barcodes), FBD-100236 (DESFire ABT), FBD-100250 (Pre-printed smartcards). Report-only — no edits made.

**Summary:** 6 genuine problems found across 211 cases (High 0 · Medium 2 · Low 4). The suite is largely coherent and well-grounded; notably the cEMV inspection cases (events 5008–5013, "cannot annul", 30s timeout, RID-list lifecycle) and the ABT-audit inspection-tap values (product `5001`, `TID "0"`, `IsInspection true`, `CardDetails`/`MaskedPan`) all check out against FBD-100716 / FBD-100658. Single-use barcode cases are correctly scoped to NIR (not Glider). Findings below are edge cases, cross-case contradictions, and a few unconfirmed values.

---

**C4103831 / C4103883** | Medium | coherence — direct cross-case contradiction | Both cases take the *same* input (an **expired Adult iLink smartcard** presented at top-up) and assert opposite outcomes. C4103831 ("Top-Up on an expired card is rejected"): "**the top-up is refused AND an expired-card message is displayed**". C4103883 ("an expired smartcard is topped up and reactivated"): "**the smartcard is returned to a valid state AND a top-up receipt is printed**". As written they cannot both be true. | Disambiguate the two expiry types: a top-up reactivating an **expired product / expired journeys** on a still-valid card medium (C4103883) is normal transit behaviour, whereas a **physically expired card medium / concession expiry** should be refused (C4103831). Reword preconds so the trigger differs, or retire one.

**C4103835** | Medium | grounding + cross-case incoherence | Title "a card-expired inspection maps DeclinedReason to 1"; expected "the audit record has **DeclinedReason set to 1** for Card Expired". Two issues: (1) The dedicated Revenue-Inspection case **C4103982** models the identical scenario (expired card tapped for inspection) as failing the **M020 integrity check → "Card Declined" screen + device event 5010** — per FBD-100716 §step 2, an expired card fails at the M020 *before* ChipDNA tokenisation, so it never obtains a FEIG token / PAR / CardDetails and would not generate a full inspection audit tap. (2) `DeclinedReason=1` is a valid enum in FBD-100658 but that taxonomy is used for the TOO/TOTO tap/journey audit, not clearly for an M020-declined card. The two cases therefore represent the same event two incompatible ways. | Reconcile C4103835 with C4103982: decide whether an expired-card inspection surfaces as device event **5010 / "Card Declined"** (per FBD-100716) or as an audit-record **DeclinedReason=1** (per FBD-100658), and confirm whether an M020-integrity failure produces an audit tap at all. Mark needs-confirmation until settled.

**C4103828** | Low | conflation / needs-confirmation | Title "a successful validation posts an ABT audit record"; case is in section "Smartcards & ABT" and the validated media are legacy **Mifare iLink/Smartpass** stored-value cards (FBD-100250), not account-based cEMV. FBD-100658 defines the "ABT" audit record as cEMV-only (`CardType` always `"emv"`, `paymentType "ABT"`). A stored-value smartcard validation flows to CloudFare/Merit but is not an "ABT tap". | Clarify the audit-record terminology (smartcard-validation audit vs cEMV ABT tap) so the assertion doesn't imply an ABT/cEMV schema for a Mifare validation.

**C4103833 / C4103834 / C4103835** | Low | coherence — mis-section / duplication | These three "ABT Tap" cases describe **cEMV inspection-tap** behaviour but live in section "Smartcards & ABT", duplicating the dedicated "Revenue Inspection (cEMV/RID)" section (e.g. C4103982 covers the same expired-card inspection; C4103980 covers the success/audit path). The audit values themselves (product `5001`, `TID "0"`, `IsInspection true`, `CardDetails`/`MaskedPan`) are correctly grounded in FBD-100658 — only their placement/overlap is the issue. | Consolidate the inspection-tap audit assertions under Revenue Inspection (cEMV/RID), or cross-reference, to avoid two sections testing the same taps.

**C4103970** | Low | grounding / needs-confirmation | "Old Barcode Redemption — a legacy BRS barcode is redeemed on Glider". FBD-100483 explicitly states **"Glider HHD does NOT use single-use functionality"**; the read specs (FBD-100483 / FBD-100167) do not describe a separate legacy "BRS" (Barcode Redemption System) redemption flow on Glider. The case may be valid against a legacy spec not in this set. | Confirm the BRS legacy-redemption flow exists as a distinct Glider HHD capability (cite the governing spec/CR); otherwise reclassify or remove.

**C4103842** | Low | coherence — title vs body | Title "outstanding sales are annulled at sign-off" (plural, framed as a sign-off sweep); preface and step annul only "**the last transaction**" / "the sale" during the sign-off flow — a single just-completed sale, not multiple outstanding sales. | Align the title with the single-last-sale scope (e.g. "the last sale is annulled during sign-off"), or broaden the body if a multi-sale sweep is actually intended.
