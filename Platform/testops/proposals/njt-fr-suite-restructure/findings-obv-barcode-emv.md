# Coverage classification — NJT Fare Register, OBV Barcode / EMV Transactions (§6.1–6.2)

**Existing cases:** `reports/uk-bus-projects/njt-farebox-and-register-replacement/2026-08-13/area-cases/obv-barcode-emv.md` (199 cases)
**Spec:** `knowledge/njt/specs/fs002-obv-barcode-emv.md` (NJT_FRFRP_FS002 §6.1–6.2); skimmed `knowledge/njt/specs/hmi01-fare-register-hmi.md` for the Barcode Validation Screen / PayGo Transactions screens.

**Sampling approach (Barcode Products, 69 cases).** Given the volume, this is a spot-check, not row-by-row
verification: 3–4 representative codes were checked per fare category present in the dump (Bus one-way/round-trip/
10-trip/monthly — e.g. C4085477/78, C4085492/85, C4085497/93; City Subway monthly — C4085507/02; RiverLine —
C4085515/18; Rail Pass monthly — C4085530/32; HBLR monthly/combined — C4085544/46, C4085517) plus a couple of
post-2019-style combined codes (e.g. C4085510, C4085594). Each sampled case's ticket-type code and rider-class
label were checked for internal consistency (code paired with a plausible, non-"Unknown" label) — not against
the raw spec table rows p.46–49 directly (not transcribed into the knowledge note; see Gap 2 below). No case in
the dump asserts against one of the spec's "Unknown"-labelled post-2019 codes, so that specific defined-gap
question doesn't surface as a false-pass anywhere in the sample.

## Summary
- **Covered:** the large majority of both Barcode and EMV Transaction-Process/Auditing/Receipt/Spotter-Display
  behaviour, and both HMI screen families (Barcode Validation, PayGo Transactions). Barcode Products (69 cases)
  covered per the sampling approach above with no inconsistency found in-sample.
- **Missing (drafted):** 4 — (1) barcode multi-passenger × C-then-modify path (3 new cases: Continue Trip,
  Transfer, Override), (2) EMV Waiting-for-EMV-Validation-Information Student-disabled-on-route exclusion (1 new
  case).
- **Partial (drafted):** 1 — barcode receipts missing the `*` OBV-validation prefix and `$F1`-zone-source
  assertions (1 new case covering both facts on one accepted-barcode-receipt scenario).
- **Gaps logged to the register (not drafted, not guessed):** the knowledge note's 4 named gaps (stale §6.1.2.1
  cross-reference, "Unknown"-labelled post-2019 barcode codes, zone display-vs-audit-field divergence, EMV OW/CT
  toggle §4.1.3 dependency) plus one newly-identified ambiguity (barcode automatic-ack + Override Required
  interaction — spec doesn't say how/whether these combine).
- **Flagged, not gap-registered:** a suspected content defect in two existing EMV auditing cases (C4085106
  asserts "audited as Cash" instead of "the paper-based equivalent product"; C4085107 appears to be a copy/paste
  of C4085106 rather than a genuine Automatic-mode case) — recommend a defect-review pass, not a Q&A gap.
