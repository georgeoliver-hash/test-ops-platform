==== 4103578 Fare-Stage Selection — the POS shows and prints the Fare Stage name, not the stop name
REFS: FBD-100207
PREFACE: This test is to confirm the POS displays and prints the Fare Stage name rather than the individual stop name.
PRECONDS: **GIVEN** an operator is signed on to a POS building a bus fare
**AND** the POS presents fares by Fare Stage, not by individual stop
**AND** example: on route 72b the boarding stop Moygashel Busby Shop belongs to a Fare Stage (Fare Stage name taken from the fare triangle — confirm against the fares export)
 STEP content: **WHEN** the operator selects the boarding and alighting stages and issues the ticket
 STEP expected: **THEN** the result screen shows the Fare Stage name, not the stop name
**AND** the printed ticket shows the Fare Stage name, not the stop name
EXPECTED: The POS shows and prints the Fare Stage name rather than the underlying stop name.
